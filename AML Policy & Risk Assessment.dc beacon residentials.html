1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Beacon Residentials Ltd ("the Business") identifies, assesses and manages the risk of money laundering and terrorist financing arising from its lettings activity, and confirms the procedures followed to meet the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 ("the Regulations"). It applies to all lettings work carried out by the Business, whether Let-Only or Full Management.

The Business does not hold client money (rent is paid directly from tenant to landlord; the Business invoices the landlord separately for its fees) and does not currently operate a Client Money Protection scheme. This materially reduces — but does not remove — money laundering risk, since letting agency business itself is a regulated activity under the 2017 Regulations regardless of whether client money is held.

4. Standard Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

Applied to every landlord before instruction is accepted and the management agreement is signed:

  1. Identity verification — current passport, driving licence, or biometric residence permit. Verified in NexLet by AI document-read cross-checked against the name supplied.
  2. Address verification — utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 3 months.
  3. Proof of ownership / right to let — Land Registry title register, mortgage statement, or council tax bill in the landlord's name; verified against the identity above.
  4. Sanctions screening — every landlord (and any joint owner or company director) is screened by name against the UK Sanctions List (the consolidated gov.uk list, successor to the OFSI Consolidated List since 28 January 2026) before instruction. Result and timestamp are logged.
  5. Corporate landlords — certificate of incorporation plus identity documents for all directors and beneficial owners holding 25% or more.

5. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Enhanced measures apply where any of the following are present:

  • The landlord is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP), or a family member/known close associate of one.
  • The landlord is resident outside the UK (Non-Resident Landlord).
  • Ownership is held through a company registered outside the UK, or a trust structure.
  • The sanctions screen returns a possible match that cannot immediately be ruled out.
  • Funds for the property's purchase, or the rental income destination, appear inconsistent with the landlord's stated circumstances.

EDD means: obtaining additional independent evidence of identity, establishing source of funds/wealth where relevant, and — for any unresolved sanctions or PEP concern — the MLRO must approve the instruction before it proceeds, or decline it.

6. Sanctions compliance

Every landlord is screened against the UK Sanctions List before onboarding completes. A confirmed true match is a criminal offence to proceed with — the instruction must not go ahead, and the MLRO must report the match to the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) without delay. A possible match with low confidence is reviewed manually by the MLRO before being cleared or escalated; this review is recorded.

7. Ongoing monitoring

Landlord details are reviewed if circumstances change materially (e.g. property transferred to a company, landlord moves abroad, new joint owner added). All landlord records are re-screened against the sanctions list if any of these triggers occur, and otherwise are not routinely re-screened absent a trigger.

8. Record keeping

All CDD documents, sanctions screening results, and any SAR-related records are retained for a minimum of 5 years from the end of the business relationship, per Regulation 40. Records are stored securely within NexLet (access-controlled, owner-only) and are not shared beyond what is necessary for the letting relationship.

9. Reporting suspicious activity

Any suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing — regardless of how small — must be reported internally to the MLRO immediately and in writing. Staff must not "tip off" the person concerned. The MLRO decides whether to submit a SAR to the NCA via the online SAR portal, and keeps a written record of that decision and its reasoning.
